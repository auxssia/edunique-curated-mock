First, find out how your child learns

The SICA test (Cognitive & Intelligence Skill Test) takes 60 to 75 minutes in LearningSpace. It gives you a complete picture of who your child is: their intelligence, brain function, foundation strengths and academic gaps, along with their personality traits.

Generative AI and adaptive learning then help your child grow in every part of their education, through books, Skill Boxes and other tools.
